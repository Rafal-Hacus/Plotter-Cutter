      SVG to PLT

      Load a finished print-and-cut SVG: registration marks plus cut lines. Marks come from the regmarks layer (scpro2_regmarks), or else from the L-marks in the four corners. Cut lines are stroked shapes without fill; they’re grouped by layer, or by colour if there are no layers, and each group becomes its own PLT, positioned from the marks. Artwork, filled shapes and the print-only layer are ignored, and <use> clones are resolved.

      Overcut, blade offset and speeds come from Cut lines below; force is set per group.

        About these files

        Batch: all stickers over as many sheets as needed: one print PDF (300 dpi, a page per sheet, its ID printed next to the start arrow, where the sheet survives the cut) and the kiss and die PLTs. Identical sheets share one pair of PLTs. Copies set the amount; designs with empty copies fill the space left over on every sheet (on their own they get one full sheet each).

        The template’s layers stay unchanged. Artwork goes into its print-only layer (scpro2_printonly), so SignCut prints it but doesn’t cut it. Cut lines get their own layers, named under Cut lines.

        The PLT zip has separate kiss and die files in the same format SignCut sends to the Skycut, positioned from the registration marks. Each PLT starts with a short V cut along the printed start arrow (in the waste, outside the marks) that sets the blade’s direction before the first outline.
